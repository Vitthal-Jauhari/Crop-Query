# DELCON rejection: evidence audit and revision decisions

*Local audit of `submitted_paper/main_paper.pdf`, `crop_query_v2.tex`, source, and executed notebook outputs; 14 October 2026. Reviewer comments are confidential and remain local. The submitted files are untouched.*

## Assessment

The reviewers' novelty concern is justified. The introduction's four “contributions” list a CNN ensemble, FAISS fallback, dual-source RAG, a quantized LLaVA model, translation, and validation. These are system components; the manuscript never states a falsifiable mechanism distinguishing CropQuery from other agricultural multimodal assistants. A later F2 training run exists, but its comparison is not ready to replace the DELCON result. More urgently, the paper's “held-out” vision and advisory estimates are not supported by the executed split and evaluation protocol. Wording changes alone will not solve the scientific issue.

## Novelty and positioning

The paper's “first” statements are untenable as written. [AgriDoctor (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17044) already describes an agricultural assistant with classifier, detector, retriever, and LLM; [Vision Meets Language (2026)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42979-026-05246-4) connects visual detection, RAG, and farmer remediation. [AgroBench (ICCV 2025)](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Shinoda_AgroBench_Vision-Language_Model_Benchmark_in_Agriculture_ICCV_2025_paper.html) is a broad VLM benchmark; the project's 39-entry treatment collection should not be conflated with the whole benchmark. This is a focused check of close sources, not a systematic novelty review. The manuscript should state exactly which failure mode it addresses and show a same-case comparator.

Candidate journal question: **When the image classifier is uncertain or its label conflicts with retrieved agronomic evidence, does an explicit evidence-aware decision to answer, qualify, or defer reduce unsupported treatment claims?** This is a hypothesis for new work, not a claim about the current implementation. A smaller conference resubmission could instead present the integrated system honestly as an application study, after correcting the evaluation and adding the requested cases.

## Immediate minimum action — completed outcome

The historical final checkpoints and training manifests could not be recovered, so their reported scores remain unverified. A new split was frozen before training from the nine student-supplied image sources: 106,332 train, 6,635 validation, and 19,923 test images, with no assigned group or exact SHA-256 crossing partitions. A one-seed matched ResNet50 screen then compared CE and CE+F2 from the same base checkpoint. F2 was 0.386 percentage points lower than CE on the untouched test partition, with a test-group bootstrap 95% interval [-0.614, -0.160]. This rejects the F2 improvement claim for the tested schedule and stops expansion to additional backbones. The remaining scientific priority is independent agronomist assessment of real image-to-advice cases.
